The session, step by step

Three chapters, one foundation

Chapter 1

The prescriber-activity universe

Your input

You placed a large new dataset and asked us to teach the engine how to read it.

Our analysis

We examined it: about 1,040 gastroenterology prescribers, each with a weekly prescribing history reaching back to late 2018, covering both total prescriptions and new prescription starts, for Crohn’s disease and ulcerative colitis.

Decisions reached

Reading the weekly history

We read it most-recent-week-first, and treat the latest week as fully reported — not a partial week.

Measuring growth

Growth compares recent weeks against the matching earlier weeks (for example, the last 13 weeks versus the previous 13). When no period is named, we default to the most recent 13 weeks.

Avoiding double-counting

Drug-class totals are kept separate and excluded when adding up individual brands, so volumes are never counted twice.

Prescriber value & behaviour

Prescribers are grouped into value tiers (High / Medium / Low) and behavioural segments, so effort can focus on the highest-value and fastest-growing prescribers.

Privacy

We kept the data-privacy-preference indicator visible and in scope, rather than removing those prescribers.

Practice: every rule and assumption was logged, so results stay traceable. Parked: the meaning of certain targeting indicators, to confirm later.

Which prescribers count as in-scope, and how the sources connect, are settled once we layer in the two reference lists (Chapter 3).

Chapter 2

Confirming the method

Your input

You shared your source query — the exact logic your team uses.

Our analysis

We read it and confirmed the engine mirrors it: prescribing activity is read directly from the data, not recalculated. Because the data carries more than one Skyrizi line, this also raised a question about which Skyrizi form to use.

Decisions reached

Read directly, not recalculated

Total prescriptions and new prescription starts are taken straight from the source, exactly as your query does.

Skyrizi’s main form

We use Skyrizi’s main inflammatory bowel disease form, which carries the large majority of its activity; the separate hospital-administered starter form is tracked on its own.

Parked: whether two other brands should be read as all-forms or a specific form, to confirm later.
Chapter 3

Who’s in scope & where they practice

Your input

With the activity data in place, two reference lists were added — an official in-scope prescriber list, and an authoritative source for where each prescriber practices.

Our analysis

All three sources cover the same ~1,040 prescribers and connect through a single shared prescriber ID.

Decisions reached

Official in-scope list

Analysis is bounded to this list. Today it matches the full universe, so no prescribers are excluded yet — it is the authoritative scope if that ever narrows.

One clean record per prescriber

The location source is tidied to a single, most-complete record for each prescriber.

Master source for location

When sources disagree on where a prescriber practices, this dedicated location source is the one we trust.

One shared ID links everything

The three sources join cleanly on a single shared prescriber ID.

Universe, method, and scope-and-geography are now complete — the engine has a trustworthy, fully-documented foundation.

Every decision, in one place

The decision log

The same eleven decisions from the timeline, grouped by chapter — each with why it matters.

Ch.1 · The universe

  1. Read history latest-first; latest week is complete. Trends and the most recent week are reported accurately.
  2. Growth = recent vs matching prior weeks (default: last 13). A consistent, comparable growth measure.
  3. Exclude drug-class totals from brand sums. Volumes are never double-counted.
  4. Value tiers & behavioural segments defined. Effort can focus on high-value, fast-growing prescribers.
  5. Privacy preference kept visible, in scope. A deliberate, documented privacy stance.

Ch.2 · The method

  1. Activity read directly, not recalculated. The engine matches your own query logic exactly.
  2. Skyrizi’s main form used; starter form tracked separately. Skyrizi volume reflects its true main form.

Ch.3 · Scope & geography

  1. Analysis bounded to the official in-scope list. Results always reflect the agreed population.
  2. One clean record per prescriber. No inflated or duplicated prescriber counts.
  3. One master source for prescriber location. Geography is consistent and unambiguous.
  4. One shared ID links all three sources. Sources combine cleanly and reliably.
What this enables

A foundation the business can rely on

  • Prescriber activity and growth tracked reliably over time
  • Targeting the highest-value and fastest-growing prescribers
  • Brand views and drug-class views — without double-counting
  • Geographic views by region, district, and territory
  • Both total prescriptions and new prescription starts
  • Analysis bounded to the official in-scope prescriber list

Consistent, traceable, and free of double-counting — every answer back to a decision on the record.
