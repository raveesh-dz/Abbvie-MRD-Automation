03

The plan & the rule

★ hero

The plan needed one decision the data can't make alone: how to turn a single weekly volume into a UC/CD split. That maps to a governing business rule — already defined, so it was applied as-is.

RULE-004

Indication allocation

metricWeekly × Monthly✓ reviewed
What it does

Splits a product's full weekly volume across only the reported indication set, using that set's national monthly mix renormalised to sum to 1.

Why it exists

The weekly panel has no indication breakdown — only the monthly table does. Renormalising within the reported set guarantees the parts sum back to the true weekly total, so a UC/CD view never silently loses volume. Trade-off, stated: a subset like IBD then absorbs the whole total, overstating it versus an on-label denominator.
